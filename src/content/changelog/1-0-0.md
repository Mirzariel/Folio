## What ships

- Catalogue and gallery with metadata, across the folders and drives you nominate.
- Exact duplicate detection, judged on content rather than name, size or timestamp.
- Quarantine into a `.Folio` folder on the same drive, with restore.
- Cleanup through the Windows Recycle Bin. No permanent deletion.
- Rename in bulk, with a mandatory preview and extensions preserved.
- Organize by capture date, within the source you chose.
- Plan, approve, verify on every operation that changes a file.

## Not in this release

Import from a connected phone or camera, and Folio for macOS. Both are in development and
neither has a release date.
